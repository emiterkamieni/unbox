First public release of unbox – a menu bar app that shows what Box Drive keeps offline on your Mac and frees the space with one click.

- **See everything that is available offline**: folders and files marked “Make Available Offline” in Box Drive, grouped by top-level folder, with the space each one uses on disk.
- **Make items online-only**: uncheck them and click Apply. unbox uses Box Drive’s own “Make Available Online Only” action and removes the downloaded copies. Files stay visible in Finder and download again when opened; files with changes that were not uploaded yet are never removed.
- Sort by name or size, reveal items in Finder.
- 15 languages – follows the system language or choose one in Settings.
- Launch at login.
- Update notifications every 24 hours and in-app updates with signed packages.

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 13 or later and Box Drive.
